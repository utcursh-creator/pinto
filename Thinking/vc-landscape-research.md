# VC Landscape Research: AI Services, Forward Deployed Engineers, Implementation Gap

## Key Thesis Validated

The "Forward Deployed AI Partner" positioning is validated at the highest level of tech investing.

## a16z (Joe Schmidt)

### "Trading Margin for Moat" (June 2025)
- FDE is "the hottest job in startups right now"
- "Enterprises buying AI are like your grandma getting an iPhone: they want to use it, but they need you to set it up."
- OpenAI has 22/311 open roles in forward-deployed/solutions categories
- Forward-deployed motions will become "the default sales motion" in 2026

### "From Demos to Deals" (June 2025)
- "Creating flashy demonstrations has become trivial. Translating these into production-ready enterprise software remains exceptionally challenging."
- AI sells the work output itself, replacing labor budgets (significantly larger than software budgets)
- Token costs dropped 83% in 2 years ($30 to <$5/M tokens)

### "Oil Wells vs Pipelines" (August 2025)
- Pipeline strategy: automate the "glue work" between systems
- Does not require rip-and-replace, gains traction rapidly

### Big Ideas 2026
- "The vast majority of the AI opportunity lives OUTSIDE of Silicon Valley"
- Opportunity is "massive in traditional consulting and services industries"

### CIO Survey 2025
- Innovation budgets collapsed from 25% to 7% of LLM spend (AI is now core, not experimental)
- One CIO: "What I spent in 2023 I now spend in a week"
- AI-generated code went from 10-15% to nearly 90% within 12 months (one CTO)

## Y Combinator

### Spring 2026 RFS: "AI-Native Agencies"
- "Instead of selling software to customers, charge way more by using the software yourself and selling the finished product at 100x the price."
- "Agencies of the future will look more like software companies, with software margins"
- 67/144 YC Spring 2025 startups focused on AI agents (~47%)

### Lightcone Podcast: "Vertical AI Agents Could Be 10X Bigger Than SaaS"
- AI replaces software AND labor costs. TAM is fundamentally larger than SaaS.
- Garry Tan: "YC wants founders who treat AI agents not as features but as the core operating system of brand-new companies."

## Sequoia Capital

### "AI's Trillion-Dollar Opportunity" (May 2025)
- "AI is attacking both software AND services markets simultaneously"
- Tools → copilots → autopilots continuum
- "The greatest value will be created at the application layer"

### "AI in 2026: A Tale of Two AIs" (Dec 2025)
- "Big enterprises are struggling to implement AI in-house, which is leading to fatigue and disappointment."
- "$0 to $1B club" emerging in 2026
