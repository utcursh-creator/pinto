## What OTT is (the product)
A calibrated SOP-COMPLIANCE video-editing factory for high-volume creator/UGC/product reels. Code at `/Users/utkarsh/ott` (docs: OTT-SYSTEM.md, SWAGZ-EDIT-GUIDELINES.md). Three moving parts:
1. **Pre-processor** (entirely customized): download, reframe 1080x1920, scrape products, transcribe/route audio, vision-map each product to its on-screen window, detect creator graphics.
2. **Renderer** (Hyperframes): composites typography supers (brand/product/USP) to a strict SOP, license-free audio, masks the creator's baked-in intro/outro graphics.
3. **Post-processor**: dense QC to the brand guideline (super never covers product/face, big/elderly-readable, no fabricated brands, sentence-case, safe areas).
- The edit SOP is GENERAL-PURPOSE. It is a SERVICE: each new client's editing job gets mapped + the system CALIBRATED to their SOP in ~1 WEEK, then it executes end to end at volume with QC.
- Differentiator: NOT generic AI editing (Opus Clip / Fiverr). The moat = the encoded client edit-SOP + vision product-mapping + QC discipline = their house standard enforced identically at volume.
- Proof/reference client: **PulpKey** (Mumbai influencer-marketing co) - runs in production for their product-reel editing.
