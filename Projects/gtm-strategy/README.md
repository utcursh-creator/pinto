# JTM Strategy

Figuring out how to convert strong positioning into booked meetings.

## The Problem
- Positioning is clear (Forward Deployed AI Partner for agencies)
- Proof is strong (50+ implementations, Loom, UpGrad, 2000+ reviews)
- But meetings aren't getting booked
- The gap is in distribution and conversion, not in the offer

## What Needs to Happen
- Map the path from "ICP sees content" to "meeting gets booked"
- Identify which channels, touchpoints, and sequences actually work
- Test and iterate on outreach / inbound mechanisms
- The content engine feeds this — authority content creates inbound pull

## Key Questions to Answer
- Where does the ICP hang out? (Ben's community is one — where else?)
- What's the trigger that makes them reach out?
- What's the current conversion bottleneck?
- Is the entry point (2-week sandbox) clear enough in content?
