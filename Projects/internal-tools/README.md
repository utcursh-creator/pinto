# Internal Tools & Apps

Mapping own internal processes and building AI solutions around them.

## Purpose
- Not for selling — for own operational efficiency and growth
- Identify bottleneck processes in daily workflow
- Build AI solutions that remove friction
- Side benefit: proof of concept material for content and client conversations

## Approach
1. Map internal processes (what do I do repeatedly?)
2. Identify which ones have clear patterns and high time cost
3. Build lightweight AI solutions around them
4. Document what works — becomes content and case study material
