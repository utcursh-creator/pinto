# Copywriting Craft Foundation (for the extractor + writer agent)

Proven direct-response frameworks, mapped to Utkarsh's short-form organic B2B content
(Twitter, AI/process-automation agency operators + business owners). This is the
PERSUASION ARCHITECTURE half of the writer agent. Pair with:
- `writing-style-analysis.md` (his thinking pipeline)
- `voice-samples.md` (his actual posts)
- `proof-bank.md` (his build proof, anonymized)

Per Utkarsh: the system is a RESEARCHER + writer. Extraction is not summarizing; it
reasons, selects angle, researches deep. These frameworks are reasoning inputs, NOT a
template to stamp on every post (his standing anti-determinism rule).

## I. The 6 highest-leverage transferable principles

1. **Enter the conversation already in the reader's mind** (Robert Collier). Open at the thought the reader is already having, not at your topic. U's "If you are a domain expert" (identity) and "I like reading surveys" (shared curiosity) are both this.
2. **Channel existing mass desire, do not create it** (Eugene Schwartz, Breakthrough Advertising). Live desires for this audience: get clients results, find the unoccupied niche, do not waste spend on hype. Every angle attaches to one.
3. **Match the market's AWARENESS stage** (Schwartz, 5 stages: Unaware -> Problem -> Solution -> Product -> Most-Aware). Dictates how far back you start teaching. Claude Skills piece = Unaware (teach via analogy first); PwC piece = Problem-Aware (move fast to system analysis).
4. **Match the market's SOPHISTICATION stage** (Schwartz, 5 stages). The B2B AI market is LATE-stage: generic claims ("AI will transform your business") are burned out. Late-market rule: shift from claim to MECHANISM (explain the why nobody else explains) and IDENTIFICATION (mirror lived experience). This is why U's circular-spending breakdown lands.
5. **Specificity creates belief** (Hopkins, Caples, Bencivenga). "People buy specifics, not generalities." Exact numbers, sample sizes, named entities, dollar figures do the persuasive work ("4,454 CEOs across 95 countries", "55% vs ~20%, a 3x gap").
6. **The slippery slide** (Joseph Sugarman). Each unit's only job is to pull the reader into the next. "The sole purpose of the first sentence is to get the second read." U's single-sentence-paragraph rhythm is a slide built for the feed; greased by open loops + tying to current news.

Supporting: One Big Idea (one post = one idea), proof/credibility stacking (Bencivenga), soft PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve), AIDA (treat Action as a thought-shift not a click).

## II. Angle-selection / nugget-extraction framework

Given a raw transcript (e.g. a McKinsey/PwC AI report), how a master mines it. One source yields MANY angles; rank them.

1. **Research-extract, do not summarize** (Bencivenga "research with a hand truck"; Georgi RMBC = Research step). Pull every hard number, sample size, named entity, dollar figure, and CONTRADICTION. The data point that contradicts the prevailing narrative is raw angle ore.
2. **Find the MECHANISM** (Georgi RMBC = Mechanism; Schwartz late-sophistication). For each candidate ask: what is the underlying WHY nobody has named? The mechanism, not the stat, is the One Big Idea. A report has 10 stats but only 2-3 real mechanisms = the strongest angles.
3. **Score each angle on 4 filters:**
   - Desire-fit: attaches to a live mass desire? (Schwartz)
   - Awareness-distance: how much teaching before the reveal? Short = single post; long = thread/long-form. (Schwartz awareness)
   - Freshness vs sophistication: burned-out claim, or mechanism/identification the market has not heard? Drop burned-out. (Schwartz sophistication)
   - Decision-relevance: does the reader's world change / does it connect to a bet they are making? (U's editorial filter). If it only confirms a belief, cut it.
4. **One source -> many ranked angles.** From one report: the contradiction angle, the mechanism angle, the unoccupied-space angle, the what-winners-do angle. Each enters the conversation at a different point. Rank by the 4 filters; ship highest first.

## III. Draft-structure framework (reasoning sequence, NOT template)

Varies per idea (never repeat structural patterns post to post). The masters give the moves; the idea decides order and emphasis.

1. **Entry** (Collier + Sugarman line 1). Open inside the reader's existing thought. Pick the mode the idea needs: identity-filter, shared curiosity, contradiction, tension. First line's only job: earn the second.
2. **Calibrate the teach to awareness** (Schwartz). New concept = teach simply via analogy first. Familiar = move fast to the system. Do not over/under-teach.
3. **Build with specifics + mechanism, soft-PAS the tension** (Hopkins/Caples specifics; Bencivenga proof; PAS). Lay hard numbers as you go; show the broken system and let the reader feel the cost WITHOUT manufactured urgency; stack proof so each claim raises belief for the next.
4. **The earned reveal** (Schwartz One Big Idea; U's "aha"). Name the mechanism/idea ONLY after the evidence makes it feel discovered. Stated upfront = cliche; earned = realization.
5. **Land on the reader, not on you** (AIDA Action reframed). End pointed at the gap / unoccupied space / the bet the reader now sees differently. Describe the gap clearly enough that whoever needs it filled knows who wrote the post. Soft signal only ("Save this"); never "DM me".

Offer-lens (Hormozi $100M Offers value equation: Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort)): a THINKING tool only, for "where the value is" angles. Do not bolt onto a post.

## IV. What to DROP for organic B2B (explicit)

- Hard CTA / the close ("buy now", "book a call", "DM me"). Biggest difference from a sales letter. Replace with landing on the reader.
- Manufactured urgency / scarcity as a pressure device. (True timing like "the window is early" is fine; it is accurate, not coercive.)
- The long pitch / offer stack / risk reversal / guarantee. Sales-letter machinery, alienating in organic.
- Hype claims + superlatives. Late-sophistication audience is immune; repels practitioners.
- Open loops as clickbait (tease-and-withhold). Sugarman loops are fine only when the payoff lands in the same post.
- Rigid formula / repeated structure. Kills the voice (U's standing rule).

KEEP: enter the conversation, channel desire, awareness/sophistication calibration, specificity, proof-stacking, slippery-slide momentum, One Big Idea, earned reveal. DROP: anything whose job is to CLOSE or PRESSURE rather than earn attention and belief.
