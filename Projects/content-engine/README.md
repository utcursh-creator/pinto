# Content Engine

Building authority through operational depth. Two channels, one voice.

## Channels

### Ben's AI Accelerator Community (benai.co)
- Business owners with domain expertise — NOT tech-savvy
- They want to integrate AI into their business OR productize their expertise
- Format: community posts / articles (longer form)
- Content must be cautious in word choice — simple, no jargon, no frameworks

### LinkedIn
- Public authority building. Needs consistent cadence.
- Format: short posts + articles/newsletters
- Content style: practitioner authority, data-backed, contrarian structure

## Content Framework: 1:1:1
1 Awareness + 1 Nurture + 1 Convert, 3x/week. Internal planning only — never visible in content.
