## 1. Domain Experts + AI: The Key Differentiator in 2026

### McKinsey: Only 5% See Bottom-Line Gains (Feb 2026)

- **Stat**: While 98% of corporate executives report implementing some form of AI, only ~5% of firms report bottom-line gains from their AI investments.
- **Stat**: Less than 20% of AI implementations are deployed at scale.
- **Stat**: Organizations achieving 5%+ EBIT impact from AI represent only ~6% of respondents. These "high performers" redesign workflows and invest in transformative innovation, not just tool adoption.

## 3. AI Implementation Failure Rates 2026

### MIT: 95% of GenAI Pilots Fail

- **Stat**: 95% of generative AI pilots fail to deliver measurable impact on the P&L.
- **Stat**: Only ~5% of AI pilot programs achieve rapid revenue acceleration.
- **Methodology**: 150 interviews with leaders, survey of 350 employees, analysis of 300 public AI deployments.
- **Key Finding**: Specialized vendors succeed ~67% of the time. Internal builds succeed only ~33% of the time.
- **Key Finding**: Biggest ROI is in back-office automation (eliminating BPO, cutting agency costs), not the sales/marketing tools that get most of the budget.
- **Root Cause**: "Not the quality of the AI models, but the 'learning gap' for both tools and organizations."
- **Source**: MIT NANDA Initiative, "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025" (Published August 2025)
- **URL**: https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/ (Fortune coverage)
- **Charts**: YES - the original MIT report has data visualizations
- **Screenshottable**: Yes

### RAND Corporation: 80% AI Project Failure Rate

- **Stat**: More than 80% of AI projects fail -- double the failure rate of non-AI IT efforts.
- **Root Causes**: (1) Stakeholders miscommunicate the problem to be solved, (2) Organizations lack necessary training data, (3) Focus on latest tech rather than solving real problems.
- **Methodology**: Interviews with 65 data scientists/engineers with 5+ years experience.
- **Source**: RAND Corporation, "The Root Causes of Failure for Artificial Intelligence Projects"
- **URL**: https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA2680-1.html
- **URL (PDF)**: https://www.rand.org/content/dam/rand/pubs/research_reports/RRA2600/RRA2680-1/RAND_RRA2680-1.pdf
- **Charts**: YES - the full RAND report has data tables and visualizations
- **Screenshottable**: Yes

### Gartner: 40%+ of Agentic AI Projects Will Be Canceled by 2027

- **Stat**: Over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027 due to escalating costs, unclear business value, or inadequate risk controls.
- **Stat**: Only 130 of thousands of vendors claiming "agentic AI" capabilities are legitimate (massive "agent washing").
- **Stat**: AI agents are currently at "peak of inflated expectations" and will enter "trough of disillusionment" throughout 2026.
- **Stat**: 19% of orgs made significant agentic AI investments, 42% conservative, 31% wait-and-see (Jan 2025 poll of 3,412 attendees).
- **Source**: Gartner (June 2025)
- **URL**: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027

## 5. AI Commoditization 2026

### a16z: "LLMflation" -- 1,000x Cost Decline in 3 Years

- **Stat**: LLM inference costs dropped by a factor of **1,000x in 3 years** (GPT-3 at $60/M tokens in 2021 -> $0.06/M tokens in 2025).
- **Stat**: For equivalent performance, inference cost is decreasing by **10x every year**.
- **Cost Drivers**: Better GPU cost/performance (Moore's Law), model quantization (16-bit to 4-bit), architectural improvements.
- **Source**: a16z, "LLMflation" analysis by Martin Casado
- **URL**: https://a16z.com/llmflation-llm-inference-cost/
- **Charts**: YES - the defining chart on LLM price decline, log-scale cost curve
- **Screenshottable**: Yes, this is one of the single best charts for this narrative

### Epoch AI: LLM Inference Price Trends

- **Stat**: Across all benchmarks, prices have declined between **9x and 900x per year**, with a median of **50x per year**.
- **Stat**: The price to achieve GPT-4 performance on PhD-level science questions fell by **40x per year**.
- **Stat**: Post-January 2024 acceleration: median rate increased from 50x/year to **200x/year**.
- **Source**: Epoch AI, "LLM Inference Price Trends" (2025)
- **URL**: https://epoch.ai/data-insights/llm-inference-price-trends
- **Charts**: YES - interactive data visualizations, benchmark-specific price curves
- **Screenshottable**: Yes, excellent interactive charts

### Recent Price War Evidence

- **Stat**: GPT-4 equivalent performance: $20/M tokens (late 2022) -> **$0.40/M tokens** (2025).
- **Stat**: Anthropic cut prices by 67%. Google slashed rates by 70-80%. OpenAI has repeatedly reduced costs.
- **Stat**: DeepSeek R1 entered at $0.55/$2.19 per million tokens -- undercutting competitors by ~90%.
- **Key Quote**: "China is commoditizing AI faster than the West can monetize it."
- **URL**: https://medium.com/coding-nexus/ai-costs-are-going-down-where-is-market-going-abb054a6715a

### Where Value Concentrates (The Application Layer)

- **Key Insight**: "The AI model itself is no longer a defensible competitive advantage. When any competitor can access a similarly powerful model via an API call, the strategic battleground shifts from the algorithm to what surrounds it."
- **Three value concentration points**:
  1. **Proprietary Data**: "An AI trained on a decade of proprietary customer service logs, exclusive supply chain data, or unique scientific research will deliver insights no generic model can replicate."
  2. **Workflow Integration**: "True value is unlocked when AI is invisibly woven into core business operations."
  3. **Distribution + User Relationships**: "If models head toward commodity, strategic value shifts to whoever controls the integration layer and user relationship."
- **Source**: MixFlow AI analysis + multiple 2026 sources
- **URL**: https://mixflow.ai/blog/ai-models-commoditization-second-order-effects-2026/
- **Charts**: Some diagrams

### SaaS Valuation Impact

- **Key Trend**: AI commoditization is reshaping SaaS valuations. Companies without native AI/agentic capabilities struggle to find VC dollars at any stage.
- **URL**: https://livmo.com/blog/ai-impact-saas-valuations-2026/

## Narrative Summary for Content

The data tells a clear story for domain experts in 2026:

1. **AI tools are commoditizing fast** (1,000x cost decline in 3 years). The model is not the moat. Technical skill alone is not the moat.

2. **95% of enterprise AI pilots are failing** (MIT) because they lack domain context, workflow integration, and organizational understanding. The 5% that succeed have domain experts driving the implementation.

3. **70% of AI value comes from people and process transformation** (BCG), not from the algorithm. Domain expertise IS the value layer.

4. **The market is shifting from SaaS to "services as software"** (a16z, Foundation Capital, Bessemer). The addressable market is $6 trillion (services) vs $300 billion (software). Domain experts who understand service delivery are positioned to capture this.

5. **Vertical AI rewards domain insiders** (Bessemer, Euclid). Healthcare AI companies with domain expertise are hitting $100M ARR in under 5 years. The best vertical AI firms show $500K-$1M ARR per employee.

6. **Every major analyst firm** (Gartner, McKinsey, Deloitte, BCG, PwC, Forrester) is saying the same thing: the bottleneck is not technology -- it is domain understanding, workflow redesign, and organizational change.

The bottom line: If you have 10 years of domain expertise in healthcare, legal, finance, supply chain, or any complex industry -- you are sitting on the most valuable asset in the AI economy. The technology is cheap and getting cheaper. Your knowledge of how things actually work is irreplaceable.
